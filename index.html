Sortkeep – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Sortkeep (“the app”) is published by ShadowAI (“we”), an independent developer, who is the data controller for the processing described here. You can reach us at the email address in the Contact section. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: your photos never leave your device.

Photos and videos

The app asks for permission to read your photos and videos so you can review and delete them. Everything — including thumbnails, duplicate and blur detection — is processed on your device. We do not upload, copy or share your photos or videos, and we have no account system or servers that receive them. Photos are only deleted when you confirm a deletion.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps small local records so it works between sessions: which photos you kept, pending deletions, simple statistics (number of sessions, photos deleted, space freed) and library size snapshots. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob and, through AdMob mediation, partner ad networks. These providers may collect and use data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time from “Privacy settings” in the app. You can also reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

More information: How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Legal basis and your choices

Processing of your photos happens only on your device, at your request, to provide the features you use. Advertising data is processed by Google only with your consent where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland). You can withdraw consent at any time from “Privacy settings” in the app. Google may process advertising data outside the EEA under its own safeguards; see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Settings file

On launch the app downloads a small public settings file (sortkeep-config.json, hosted on GitHub Pages) that tunes how often ads appear. The request is an ordinary web request: it contains no account, device identifier or information about your photos. GitHub, like any web host, sees the IP address of the request.

Purchases

The optional “Remove ads” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase is active on your Google account.

Retention

We keep no personal data on any server. Data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Data held by Google is subject to Google’s retention policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you outside your device, most of these are exercised directly in the app or in your Android and Google account settings; for anything else, contact us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs. Advertising is served by Google AdMob and the ad networks mediated through it (currently Liftoff, Meta Audience Network, Pangle and DT Exchange, when enabled), which may process advertising data as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
